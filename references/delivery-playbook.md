# Delivery Playbook

How to open, engage, run Q&A, and close a live workshop. The teaching part was done when the slides were finished — the call itself is performance: part radio personality, part educator, part performer.

## Pre-Call

- **On the call 20 minutes early.** Every time. Verifies nothing breaks, and de-stresses the "let them in" moment.
- Get in state: headphones, your pump-up song, coffee before. The prep is done — now you perform.
- **Start recording BEFORE letting anyone in**, then double-check it during the warm-up.

## Warm-Up (first 5–10 min, before the "real" start)

- People are always late. Host ad hoc: greet by name ("How are you doing, Matt? What's been happening?"). Their name is their favorite word — use it.
- This is also your recording sanity-check window.

## Scripted Opening Line

Script your exact first sentence ("All right team, welcome to…"). Three reasons: it kills the adrenaline spike, it gives the replay a clean edit point, and you never fumble the start. Use the same one every time — on stage too.

## During

- **Why section = your runway.** The first 15 minutes are always rocky; the Why eases you in. You'll hit rhythm ~30 min in.
- **Re-engage between chapters:** "How's everyone feeling? Thumbs up?" Every thumbs-up, laugh, or chat message is a re-engagement — and your beat to check the next slide and reset.
- **Slow down.** This is not a YouTube video. You're having a conversation. Ironically you'll fumble LESS live than recording.
- **Fumbles: let it roll.** You're a car barreling down the highway — things fall out the windows, keep driving. Joke about it or pretend it didn't happen.
- Nerves are a feature, not a bug. If you feel nothing, get psychoanalyzed.

## Q&A (optional, at the end)

- **Pre-frame:** (1) "Questions related to today's lesson please." (2) "Just ask the question — I'll ask you for the context I need." (Stops life stories, keeps it moving.)
- **Manage:** have A system — hand-raise order is fine; breakout rooms or splitting the room also work. Just never a free-for-all.
- **Close — write this down:** never end abruptly. 20–30 min out, start the countdown: "Only 20 more minutes… 15… last question." Name who WILL get answered and who won't ("LJ, Tyler — throw yours in the async space, I'll get to you"). People only think about themselves; a stated end time at the start is forgotten — update them throughout.

## Recording Redundancy (never lose a lesson)

Record in up to four places; worst case, students still get something:
1. **In-camera** (SD card — even if you never use it)
2. **Audio interface** (RODECaster: internal, multi-channel — your mic and call audio on separate channels; combined audio sources always screw up)
3. **OBS** (face cam + slides + audio, your controlled master)
4. **Zoom** (the fallback)

**Q&A replay hack:** you're already recorded in OBS/camera — so PIN (don't spotlight) the student's video in Zoom. The Zoom recording then captures just them in full 1080p, the live experience doesn't change, and editing the replay gets dramatically easier.

## Tech Setups

- **Level 1 — iPhone + laptop.** Genuinely enough for most people. (iPhone-on-MacBook mount replaces the webcam.)
- **Level 2 — travel/serious:** USB mic (Shure MV7), any camera + Cam Link 4K (~$200, best cheap purchase), mini tabletop tripod (~$30). 90% of the studio experience.
- **Level 3 — studio:** audio interface (RODECaster Duo — arguably the single best purchase for an education business, buy it before a camera), camera into OBS via Cam Link, teleprompter used as a *monitor* to see attendees' faces, second monitor dedicated to full-screen slides.
- **OBS** (free, open source): three scenes — face cam / slides + face cam / iPad + face cam — switched with hotkeys. Virtual Camera feeds Zoom, so you never screen-share (lighter on the machine, seamless scene changes, and your recording doesn't depend on Zoom). Spotlight your own video at call start.
- Don't buy anything before your first workshop. The scrappy setup is even a pattern interrupt.

## One-Page Checklist (copy into the workshop folder, personalize)

```
PRE-CALL
[ ] Slides done, edited, read through 3–4× (15 min)
[ ] Opening line scripted: "____________________________"
[ ] On call 20 min early · state music · coffee
[ ] Recording started BEFORE doors open (camera / audio / OBS / Zoom)

OPEN
[ ] 5–10 min warm-up hosting, greet by name
[ ] Scripted opening line = recording start marker

DURING
[ ] Why = runway (10–15 min) · What = checklist · How = chapters w/ re-engage beats · Now = the asset
[ ] Slow down. Let fumbles roll.

Q&A + CLOSE
[ ] Pre-frame: on-topic questions, "I'll ask for context"
[ ] Hand-raise order
[ ] Countdown from 20 min out · name who's getting answered
[ ] Point unanswered questions to the async space

AFTER
[ ] Pull recordings from all sources · file replay in curriculum
[ ] Send the Now asset
```
